HOW YOU CAN HELP

It's easy to see an issue this big and hard to tackle and think that it's impossible you can make a difference. But it's actually very easy, and every little thing you do can lead to a greater impact. We can't do this without your help, and you can make a difference even from your couch, giving us five minutes of your time. See how you can help below, and reach out to us if you want to offer your support.

EDUCATE: SPREAD THE WORD

People can't help if they don't know about the issue itself. Use your social media platforms and community connections to educate people about animal cruelty and testing.

DONATE: OFFER YOUR SUPPORT

With funding, we can do so much more. Even just five dollars can make an impact. We are connected to different major organizations, and your donations can greatly aid in our cause and theirs as well.

VOLUNTEER: OFFER YOUR SERVICES

If you have invaluable skills, with technology, outreach, etc., your help would be greatly appreciated! We can make a bigger impact with your help and you can spend your time working with us to support our animal friends.

COMMUNITY: GET INVOLVED

Countless shelters and pounds are located in neighborhoods across the United States. Get involved with your local shelter. Volunteer, connect with the animals, or donate to the shelters to support animals that may have undergone testing conditions or are suffering.

ADOPT: CREATE A SAFE SPACE

After undergoing testing, many animals are sent to shelters or pounds, where they are often killed or put down. You can help by adopting or fostering these animals, providing a safe space for them to heal and be happy.

OUTREACH: FIGHT BACK

We need to fight this problem directly at the root. You can research animal testing and cruelty and reach out to local politicians, offices, and people in power. These people can help create new laws and truly fight for this issue and provide premanent solutions to animal cruelty.
