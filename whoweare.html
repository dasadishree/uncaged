Who Are We?

We're a group of dedicated students based in New York. Not only do we all love animals, we are also driven to make the world a better place, one step at a time. Animal cruelty is an important issue to use and we are doing everything we can to fight for a better future for our animal friends. All three of us, focusing on our specific interests and strengths within different fields of medicine, psychology, and engineering, are working to further our mission and broaden our reach. Our team is very passionate about animal rights, and we can't do this without all of you who are donating, helping, educating, and supporting our organization. Get in touch with us at our individual emails or at uncaged.organization@gmail.com.
