OUR MISSION

What are we trying to achieve?

Our mission is to make an impact in the fight for animal rights. We see the suffering and cruelty millions of animals endure in these industries, and our goal is help animals already impacted by healing them and getting them help, and also preventing more animals by being harmed. We can't do this without your support. Please donate or visit our Take Action page to see how else you can help.

OUR PROJECTS

To fully achieve our mission, we've divided it into specific points of focus and projects we want to follow.

If you're interested in helping out or donating to a specific project of ours, reach out to us at our email, uncaged.organization@gmail.com, or message us on our Instagram, @uncaged.organization.

Education & Awareness

52% of Americans oppose animal testing. Countless others aren't aware of the scope of animal testing that occurs behind closed laboratory doors, or the megacorporations behind animal suffering. Our goal is to educate people upon the real stories of animal testing. More awareness means more support, and these animals need all the support they can get.

Community & Support

There are many other organizations and nonprofits just like ours focused on battling animal cruelty. In addition to connecting with these organizations, we aim to collect donations and support from our communities and people to help our animals.
